Coral Springs questions, answered

How much does permanent roofline lighting cost in Coral Springs?

Most single-family homes run $2,000–$6,000 installed, depending on roofline length, stories and track type. The free design consult measures your home and prices it exactly.

When should I book holiday light installation?

Early — Broward December calendars typically fill by mid-to-late November. Packages typically run $1,500–$4,500 per season and include design, installation, takedown and storage.

Are permanent roofline lights HOA-friendly?

Usually, yes. The color-matched track is nearly invisible by day, and many HOAs treat it as architectural lighting. Your installer can confirm your neighborhood's rules.
