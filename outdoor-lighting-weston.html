Weston questions, answered

How much does permanent roofline lighting cost in Weston?

Most single-family homes run $2,000–$6,000 installed, depending on roofline length, stories and track type. Larger estate homes can run above the range. The free design consult prices your exact roofline.

Will a permanent roofline system pass Weston HOA rules?

In most communities, yes. The track is slim, color-matched and nearly invisible by day, so many HOAs treat permanent systems as architectural lighting. Your installer can confirm your community's rules before installation.

When should I book holiday light installation?

As early as possible — Broward December calendars typically fill by mid-to-late November. Packages typically run $1,500–$4,500 per season and include takedown and storage.
