Plantation questions, answered

How much does permanent roofline lighting cost in Plantation?

Most single-family homes run $2,000–$6,000 installed, depending on roofline length, stories and track type. The free design consult measures your home and prices it exactly.

Are permanent roofline lights OK in neighborhoods with HOAs?

Usually, yes. The track is slim, color-matched to your fascia and nearly invisible by day, so many HOAs treat permanent systems as architectural lighting. Your installer can confirm your community's rules.

What does a holiday light installation package include?

Design, installation, in-season service, January takedown and storage — typically $1,500–$4,500 per season. Book early: Broward calendars usually fill by mid-to-late November.
