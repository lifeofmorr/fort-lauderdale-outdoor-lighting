Questions, answered straight

How much does permanent roofline lighting cost?

Most Broward single-family homes run $2,000–$6,000 installed, by roofline length and stories. The free consult prices your exact roofline.

When should I book holiday installation?

Now, honestly. Broward installers' December calendars typically fill by mid-to-late November — early bookings get the design and dates they want.

Are permanent lights HOA-friendly?

Usually. The color-matched track is nearly invisible by day, and many HOAs treat it as architectural lighting. Your installer can confirm your community's rules.

What does landscape lighting cost?

The average local project is around $4,000, from starter designs below that to full estate and waterfront designs above it.

Do holiday packages include takedown?

Yes — design, install, in-season service, January takedown and storage are typically all in the package.
